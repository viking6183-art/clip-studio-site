Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

Summary

Clip Studio runs entirely on your own computer and has no server. Your data stays on your device and is only sent to the platforms you connect, to do what you asked: upload your clips.

What the app stores, and where

  • Account tokens: when you connect TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the access and refresh tokens those platforms issue are saved in files on your computer, inside the app's folder. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • App keys and settings you enter, such as your TikTok client key and posting preferences, are saved in the same local folder.
  • Videos and clips you process, plus a record of what was posted and when, are kept in local folders on your computer.

TikTok data

With your permission through TikTok Login Kit, the app uses:

  • user.info.basic: to identify the connected account. Before each upload it also reads that account's posting options, such as the privacy levels available and the maximum video length.
  • video.publish (Content Posting API): to upload clips you made to your own account, with the caption and privacy level you chose.

The app does not read your followers, messages, or other users' information. It does not sell, share or use TikTok data for advertising.

Data sent to third parties

Data only leaves your computer when the app talks directly to the services you connected:

  • TikTok, YouTube (Google) or Instagram (Meta), to upload your clips;
  • YouTube, to download the source videos you chose.

Each service handles that data under its own privacy policy. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and this website uses no cookies.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any account at any time, either by deleting its saved login in the app's folder or by revoking access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) or the other platform's settings.
  • Delete all locally stored data by deleting the app's folder.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age to use TikTok where you live.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
